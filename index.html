UGA Graduate Student Association · GSA Connect

A digital business card for every major.

51 free, open-source card templates for graduate and professional students. Host your CV, research and links on GitHub Pages, share by QR or NFC tap, and add your card to Apple, Google or Samsung Wallet.

  • 📇 Save-contact vCard
  • 📄 CV & publications
  • 🔳 QR + NFC
  • 👛 Apple · Google · Samsung Wallet
  • 🧩 Setup form + live preview
  • 🆓 Free hosting
  • 📶 Program an NFC card →

Three-minute start

gh repo create my-card --template ank-man/gsa-connect-entomology --public --clone
cd my-card && npm run setup     # form opens → upload photo & CV → Publish
